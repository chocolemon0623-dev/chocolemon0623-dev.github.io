MusicKit Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-06-08

MusicKit is a practice app for musicians provided by chocolemon. This policy explains what data the app uses and how it is handled.

Information We Use

  • Microphone input, used on the device for tuner pitch detection.
  • Audio and media files selected by the user, used for playback, practice tools, waveform analysis, and related on-device audio features.
  • App settings, tuner settings, metronome presets, practice logs, recent media references, entitlement state, and usage-limit counters stored on the device.
  • Advertising-related data processed by Google Mobile Ads when ads are enabled.
  • Purchase and subscription status processed by Google Play or Apple when in-app purchases are enabled.
  • Debug reports or feedback content that the user chooses to share from beta tools.

Microphone

Microphone access is used for tuner features. MusicKit processes microphone input on the device and does not intentionally upload microphone audio to our servers.

Audio Files

Audio and media files selected by the user are used for local playback and practice features. MusicKit may store local file references or temporary copies needed for app features, but it does not intentionally upload selected audio files to our servers.

Advertising

MusicKit may show ads through Google Mobile Ads. Google may process device identifiers, ad interaction data, approximate location, diagnostics, and related information according to Google's policies.

Purchases

MusicKit includes in-app purchase support. When purchases are enabled, Google Play or Apple may process transaction information, purchase identifiers, subscription status, and payment-related information according to their own policies. MusicKit stores entitlement state on the device to unlock app features.

Local Storage

MusicKit stores app preferences, presets, practice logs, media references, entitlement state, and usage counters on the device using local storage. This data remains on the device unless the user exports, shares, deletes, or uninstalls the app.

Debug Reports and Feedback

Beta tools may let the user create and share a debug report. A debug report can include app version, build time, platform, operating system version, locale, selected app preferences, entitlement plan, usage counters, and recent app logs. Feedback is shared only when the user chooses to send it through the device share sheet, a feedback form, email, or another selected channel.

Data Sharing

MusicKit does not sell personal information. Data may be processed by service providers used for ads, purchases, app distribution, beta feedback, and user-selected sharing.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Mobile Ads may process advertising and diagnostic data when ads are loaded or shown.
  • Google Play and Apple may process purchase, subscription, app distribution, and crash or diagnostic information according to their platform policies.
  • The device share sheet and user-selected apps may process exported presets, practice logs, debug reports, or feedback when the user chooses to share them.

Data Retention

Local app data remains on the device until the user deletes it or uninstalls the app. Feedback and debug reports sent by the user may be retained as needed to improve app quality, respond to requests, and resolve issues.

User Choices

Users can deny or revoke microphone, media, notification, and other permissions in system settings. Users can delete local app data by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app. Users can request deletion of feedback they submitted by contacting us.

Children's Privacy

MusicKit is not intended to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and delete it when appropriate.
